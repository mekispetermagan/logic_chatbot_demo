Semantics/evaluation/reference resolution later


Evaluation: basically what we need is reference resolution. What I'm having in mind is a very simple model of conversation state: a salience ranking. It should be of the form:
```
sR :: SalienceRanking
sR =
  [ (0, Id 4, Just (C Red))
  , (1, Id 3, Just (C Blue))
  , (1, Id 3, Just (Sh Sphere))
  , (2, Id 2, Just (C Green))
  , (2, Id 1, Just (C Green))
  , (3, Id 0, Nothing)
  ]
```
That is: recency score, identifier, nullable mentioned property.
Most recent is also most salient. "It" refers to most recent; "the..." refers to most recent compatible.

"A..." introduces a new object.
